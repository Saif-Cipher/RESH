Design system and catalog naming governance

Two structural standards must be locked before frontend development begins: visual consistency across bottle photography and clean separation between Resh Originals and designer-inspired scents.

Dual-Surface Visual System

  • Surfaces: Deep Obsidian (#0F1115) for hero, combo banner, and footer; Warm Alabaster (#FAF9F6) for product grids so amber and clear glass bottles pop cleanly.
  • Typography: High-contrast editorial serif (Playfair Display) for headlines paired with geometric sans (DM Sans) for prices, notes, and UI controls.
  • Photography Spec: Reshoot all 44 SKUs in 3:4 portrait aspect ratio on neutral stone/alabaster—retiring every WhatsApp-Image filename and WooCommerce placeholder.

SKU & Naming Standardization

  • House Originals Shelf: Elevate Pahari, Knight's Oud, Vampire Blood, and Resh Oud into a dedicated signature collection.
  • Trademark Cleanup: Rename direct designer titles (Bleu de Chanel, Gucci Bloom, Tom Ford Tobacco Vanille) to Resh house names with an "Inspired by…" subtitle.
  • Real SKU Taxonomy: Replace SKU: N/A with structured codes (e.g., RB-ORI-001-30ML) so courier and inventory sync works automatically.

Recommended tech stack and Bangladesh e-commerce integrations

Because Resh Bangladesh already runs on WordPress/WooCommerce, there are two viable paths: a **modern headless frontend (Next.js + WooCommerce REST/GraphQL)** for sub-second speed and a custom React Combo Builder, or a **clean custom WooCommerce block theme** if the team prefers staying inside WordPress admin. Both paths should wire in four essential local integrations.

Payments (MFS + COD)

Integrate SSLCommerz or direct bKash & Nagad merchant APIs alongside Cash on Delivery, with clear trust badges in the cart drawer and footer.

Courier Automation

Connect Steadfast, Pathao, or RedX APIs so confirmed orders auto-generate tracking IDs and power a live /track-order page without manual WhatsApp replies.

Ads & Analytics

Deploy Meta Pixel + Conversions API (CAPI) and GA4 e-commerce events (ViewContent, AddToCart, Purchase) to optimize Instagram and Facebook ad ROAS.
